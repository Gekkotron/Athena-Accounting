# Set a budget

Athena offers two budgeting approaches side by side: **Caps** (a loose
per-category limit the app warns you about) and **Envelopes** (strict
YNAB-style allocation of every euro before you spend it). You can switch
between them at any time — same data, different lens.

## 1. Pick an approach

Open **Budgets**. The landing screen presents the two paradigms as a pair
of tiles with their pitch:

- **Caps** — a limit per category, after-the-fact tracking.
- **Envelopes** — assign every euro of income before spending it.

Pick either tile to continue; `/budgets/caps` and `/budgets/envelopes` also
deep-link straight to a view.

## 2. Create a monthly cap per category

Open **Budgets → Caps**. At the top, the banner sums up your overall spending for the selected period. Every expense category shows its own progress bar: green while you're under the cap, orange as you approach it, red past it. Use the **Add a budget** form at the bottom of the page to set an amount on a category that doesn't have a cap yet.

On a first visit (no caps set), the *"unbudgeted categories"* section auto-expands with suggested amounts based on your spending history — pick a category and tune the suggested figure rather than starting from a blank form.

![Caps page with per-category progress bars](/img/walkthroughs/en/budget-01-plafonds.png)

## 3. Pick the period: month or year

The **Month / Year** switcher at the top of the page flips every cap between monthly and yearly tracking. Useful for seasonal categories (insurance, holidays) that a monthly cap would describe poorly. The arrows next to the displayed month navigate between periods for comparison.

## 4. Try the "Envelopes" view

The **Budgets → Envelopes** tab offers a stricter read: every category becomes an envelope that drains as you spend, and every euro of income must be assigned somewhere before you touch it. Unused envelopes can be **carried over** to the next month. This is the zero-based model for people who like allocating every euro up front.

Envelopes aren't "created" one by one — they materialize automatically as soon as a transaction lands in an expense category. If the page is empty, import a statement or categorize a few transactions first; the envelope list fills from there.

![Envelopes view](/img/walkthroughs/en/budget-02-enveloppes.png)

## 5. Track progress from the Dashboard

Back on **Dashboard**. Capped categories appear at the top of the **Insights** panel as soon as you approach or cross the threshold: "Your June spending: …", "You spent more than your income in June", etc. The category donut is still clickable — one click filters the matching transactions.

![Insights and progress on the dashboard](/img/walkthroughs/en/budget-03-dashboard-progress.png)
